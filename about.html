Our Story

From a Classroom
Idea to a Movement

The journey of CCL began with clarity and purpose. Interestingly, the original idea was a combination of consulting and psychology experience of the founding partners, later designed as a six-week training platform that would help students gain practical experience and internship opportunities across industries such as consulting, creative fields, and other career paths.
As discussions evolved, it became clear that there was a larger problem to solve. Many students lacked exposure, career direction, and practical skills needed beyond the classroom. The vision became creating a space where students could learn, build experience, and explore future opportunities in real time. Today, CCL stands for more than training; it represents possibility. The long-term vision is to build a community whose members become highly sought after, trusted for their skills, competence, and professional development.

Vision

To be Africa's most respected student consulting community producing bold, ethical, and impactful business leaders who shape the continent's future.

Mission

To equip every member with the intellectual tools, professional networks, and real-world experience needed to excel in consulting and beyond.

Our Commitment

We believe that Africa's best solutions come from Africa's best minds. CCL exists to surface, develop, and deploy that talent.
